# GlacioGuard AI — Complete Research Results Compendium (2000–2026 YTD)

This folder contains all publication-grade figures (`15` spatial maps at `300 DPI`), analytical graphs (`10` plots at `300 DPI`), structured tables (`12` tables in both `.csv` and `.md`), GeoJSON spatial layers (`7` datasets), and the downloadable PDF research report.

## 1. Spatial Map Figures (`research/figures/`)
- `fig01_study_area_and_dem.png` — Copernicus GLO-30 shaded-relief DEM, D8 Strahler drainage network (`51.9 km`), and reference settlements/glaciers.
- `fig02_terrain_and_hydrology_6panel.png` — 6-panel DEM geomorphometric & Priority-Flood hydrological derivatives (`Elevation`, `Slope`, `Aspect`, `TRI Roughness`, `Log10 Flow Accumulation`, `HAND`).
- `fig03_satellite_composites_timeline_2000_2026.png` — True-colour post-monsoon satellite composite gallery (`2000, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2015, 2020, 2024, 2026-YTD`).
- `fig04_historical_change_maps_6pairs.png` — Multi-decadal transition maps across the 6 mandated comparison epochs (`2000→2012` through `2024→2026-YTD`).
- `fig05_2013_kedarnath_disaster_impact.png` — Pre-disaster (`2011`) vs Post-disaster (`2013`) imagery, `ΔNDVI` riparian vegetation stripping map, and exposed debris/sediment transition.
- `fig06_glacial_lake_inventory_and_chorabari.png` — Mapped high-altitude water bodies (`n = 33`), GLOF susceptibility categories, and the `23.7 km` D8 flow path from Chorabari Lake.
- `fig07_glacier_snow_ice_retreat_chorabari.png` — False-colour (`SWIR1-NIR-Red`) and `NDSI` snow/ice transition across the Chorabari Glacier sub-region (`2000 → 2024`).
- `fig08_downstream_infrastructure_exposure_map.png` — OpenStreetMap infrastructure (`1,191` features) overlaid with the Chorabari → Mandakini potential exposure corridor (`236` exposed features).
- `fig09_2013_pre_during_post_satellite_and_ndwi.png` — 8-panel intra-2013 comparison (`Pre-Flood May 2013`, `During-Flood June 2013`, `Monsoon High-Flow July–Aug 2013`, `Post-Flood Sept–Nov 2013`) showing true colour and `NDWI/MNDWI` corridor water/wetness intensity.
- `fig10_chorabari_lake_pre_during_post_2013_zoom.png` — High-resolution zoom on **Chorabari Lake (Gandhi Sarovar) & Kedarnath Town** showing pre-flood lake storage, June 2013 outburst wetness, and post-breach drained lake basin.
- `fig11_mandakini_corridor_water_depth_pre_during_post.png` — 4-stage valley flow depth (`Δh` in metres) & inundation progression (`Pre-Flood Baseflow`, `16 June Rainfall Flash Flood`, `17 June Chorabari GLOF Outburst Peak`, `Post-Flood Autumn Recession`).
- `fig12_settlement_zoom_water_levels_4sites.png` — `4×3` zoomed water-level comparison (`Pre-Flood`, `During-Flood 17 June Peak`, `Post-Flood`) at **Kedarnath Township**, **Rambara Gorge**, **Gaurikund Valley**, and **Sonprayag Confluence**.
- `fig13_valley_cross_section_water_levels_2013.png` — Real Copernicus GLO-30 DEM valley cross-sections showing exact **Pre-Flood**, **16 June Rainfall Flood**, **17 June GLOF Peak**, and **Post-Flood** water-surface elevations (`WSE`, m a.s.l.), depths (`Δh`, m), and wetted corridor widths (`m`).
- `fig14_water_difference_maps_pre_vs_during_vs_post.png` — Differential water-index maps (`During − Pre`, `Post − During`) and peak water-level surge (`ΔWSE`).
- `fig15_3d_perspective_mandakini_flood_inundation_2013.png` — 3D topographic surface perspective comparing the Pre-Flood baseflow channel (`~1.8 m`) against the 17 June 2013 Peak Inundation Envelope (`~17.5 m`).
